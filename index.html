I'm an interaction designer with 10+ years of experience making complex products feel simple. My focus is financial services — life insurance, private pension, and occupational benefits — where clear, trustworthy UX isn't just nice to have, it's essential.

Currently at Liechtenstein Life Prosperity, designing the Prosperity app and digital insurance products for policyholders and sales partners across Europe. Before that, at XEMPUS, building the B2B platform for occupational pension consulting used by advisors and HR teams across Germany. Earlier in digital health at Temedica and greentech at Energie Zukunft Schweiz.

I work best inside cross-functional teams, close to product and engineering — advocating for the user perspective and turning it into decisions that hold up in production.

What I do

Areas of expertise

01

Product Discovery

Translating ambiguous problems into clear design directions. I facilitate workshops, align stakeholders, and help teams build the shared understanding needed to move fast and build the right thing.

02

UX Research & Validation

Running usability studies, synthesising usage data, and turning insight into actionable decisions. I make research a continuous part of the design process, not a one-off exercise.

03

Design Systems

Building scalable, component-based systems that align design and engineering, reduce inconsistency, and accelerate product delivery — without sacrificing quality or craft.

04

Prototyping & Handoff

From rough sketches to interactive prototypes. With a background in frontend development, I bridge design intent and what actually ships — reducing back-and-forth in the build phase.
